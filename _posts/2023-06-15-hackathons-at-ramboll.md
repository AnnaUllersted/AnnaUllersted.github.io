Hackathons are a fast way to test ideas, get colleagues hands-on with new tools, and build closer collaboration with clients. At Ramboll I have organised hackathons both internally and together with clients, where cross-disciplinary teams spend a short, intense period turning a real challenge into a concept or prototype.

## Ramboll Computational Design and Sustainability Ideation Challenge 2022

The first challenge brought together Ramboll colleagues across disciplines to develop ideas at the intersection of computational design and sustainability. Clients were part of the jury: teams pitched their concepts to the Danish Road Directorate (Vejdirektoratet) and AP Pension, alongside an audience vote.

Two ideas came out on top:

- **Optimaps – Roads for the future** (Vejdirektoratet's vote and the audience vote): a tool that helps road designers choose the most sustainable alignment of a route from A to B.
- **Early material choice dialogue assistance** (AP Pension's vote): a concept for a tool that shows designers and clients how material choices affect both environmental and social sustainability on a project.

## Ramboll × Trafikverket Hackathon, Stockholm 2023

A 30-hour hackathon together with the Swedish Transport Administration (Trafikverket), with Ramboll colleagues from the UK, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Denmark, Germany and India. Five teams worked on site in Stockholm and one team joined from Gurgaon, India.

The teams tackled three challenges from Trafikverket's work towards fossil-free construction:

- Real-time monitoring for sustainable construction
- Optimised mass balancing – planning the movement of cut and fill
- Simulation models for charging a fully electrified construction site

The teams pitched their solutions to a jury from Trafikverket and Ramboll. In its closing remarks, the jury said the teams were not just talking about the workforce of the future but already doing the work of a carbon-emission-free future.

## What hackathons bring

- **Innovation:** real challenges turned into concepts and prototypes in days, not months.
- **Adoption:** colleagues get hands-on experience with computational tools and methods.
- **Client collaboration:** clients take part directly, as challenge owners and jury, which turns ideas into conversations about future projects.
