A personal project built from scratch in a small team. Motiknit is an online tool that turns any uploaded image into a knitting pattern, generated to your own gauge and needle setup.

## What it does

Upload an image of a motif, set your desired height, stitch and row gauge, knitting method and the tool generates a matching knitting pattern and a recipe to follow.
