I'm a bridge engineer at Ramboll (since 2021, MSc Structural Engineering, Aarhus University), working on pedestrian and larger prestressed steel and concrete bridges. Alongside standard design work, I build software tools that automate structural engineering workflows — from parametric finite element models to Rhino/Grasshopper-based generation of complex bridge geometry.

I'm also second chair of the Computational Design Network Denmark (CDND).

I'm interested in tools that sit at the intersection of structural engineering and computational design — moving structural engineering software from repetitive manual analysis toward parametric, automated, and interactive workflows.
